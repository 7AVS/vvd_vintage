# AI Presentation Writing Assistant Research

This document summarizes the best practices and workflows for using AI to create presentations.

## Two Main Approaches

There are two primary ways to use AI for presentation writing:

*   **All-in-One AI Presentation Makers:** These are dedicated tools that handle everything from content generation to design. You provide a prompt, and the tool generates a complete presentation.
    *   **Examples:** [Gamma](https://gamma.app/), [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/), [Slidesgo](https://slidesgo.com/), [Canva AI](https://www.canva.com/ai-presentation-maker/), [Plus AI](https://www.plusdocs.com/plus-ai-presentation-maker)
*   **Custom Agents with LLMs:** This approach involves using a Large Language Model (LLM) to generate the content for your presentation, and then using a separate tool (like PowerPoint or Google Slides) to create the slides. This gives you more control over the final output, but it also requires more work.

## Best Practices for Both Approaches

No matter which approach you choose, here are some best practices to follow:

*   **Start with a Detailed Prompt:** The more information you give the AI, the better the results will be.
*   **Use AI as a Starting Point:** Always review and refine the AI-generated content.
*   **Focus on the Visuals:** Use images, charts, and graphs to illustrate your points.
*   **Keep it Simple:** Use clear and concise language.
*   **Practice, Practice, Practice:** Rehearse your presentation until you're comfortable with the material.

## Workflow for All-in-One AI Presentation Makers

1.  **Choose a Tool:** Select a tool from the list above.
2.  **Provide a Prompt:** Enter a detailed prompt.
3.  **Generate the Presentation:** Let the AI create the presentation.
4.  **Refine the Output:** Review and edit the presentation.
5.  **Download or Present:** Download the presentation or present it from the tool.

## Workflow for Custom Agents with LLMs

1.  **Generate the Content:** Use an LLM to generate the text for your presentation.
2.  **Choose a Presentation Tool:** Open your preferred presentation tool.
3.  **Create the Slides:** Create a new slide for each main point.
4.  **Add the Content:** Copy and paste the AI-generated content into your slides.
5.  **Add Visuals:** Find or create visuals to illustrate your points.
6.  **Design the Slides:** Choose a theme and a layout.
7.  **Refine and Practice:** Review and practice your presentation.
