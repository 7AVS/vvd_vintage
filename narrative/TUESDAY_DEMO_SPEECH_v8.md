# Vintage Curves Automation: Team Demo Speech (Version 8)

> **Date:** Tuesday Demo
> **Duration:** ~12-15 minutes
> **Audience:** Team + directors (they know vintage, don't teach)
> **Generated:** 2026-01-26

---

## Full Spoken Delivery

---

**[Settle. Eye contact. Begin.]**

We deliver vintage curves. That's part of what this team does.

[pause]

What I want to show you today is an approach to how we deliver them - and how that process can integrate with the broader framework we're building.

[pause]

---

### The Engine

This is the vintage engine I've been working on.

[pause]

It produces the curves, the lift calculations, the confidence intervals - the deliverables our stakeholders need to make decisions.

[pause]

The architecture has four layers.

**Experiment Metadata** - who's in the test. Pulls from existing sources.

**Campaign Metadata** - what we're measuring. Currently configured per campaign.

**Success Definitions** - how we define success. Currently configured per campaign.

**Client Journey** - what customers did. Pulls from existing sources.

[pause]

The engine sits beneath these layers. It takes whatever they feed it and calculates the output.

[pause]

---

### How It Connects

Here's what I want to highlight.

[pause]

This engine is built to integrate with Roy's Super Fact framework.

The experiment metadata aligns with those pillars. The success definitions are structured to eventually connect with the Success Library Daniel is building. The output format is designed to work with however we decide to deliver - whether that's enterprise dashboards or something in-house.

[pause]

It's not a standalone tool. It's designed to plug into what we're building as a team.

[pause]

---

### Where We Are

Six campaigns are running through the engine today. VCN, VDA, VDT, VUI, VUT, VAW.

[pause]

Two output tracks.

Track A - enterprise dashboard integration. We're working with Frank and Kelvin's teams on how that looks.

Track B - an in-house visualization. Deployed now. Helps us see the curves quickly while we figure out the enterprise path.

[pause]

---

### How the Team Contributes

**[This is the core message.]**

This isn't meant to be my project that I hand off.

[pause]

The way this is structured, there are clear places where others can contribute.

[pause]

Success definitions - as we define metrics for new campaigns, those definitions can feed into the broader library. That's a handshake with Daniel's work.

Campaign configurations - as we standardize how we set up campaigns, that knowledge becomes reusable. That helps everyone who runs vintage.

The codebase itself - Akash is setting up the GitHub repository. The intention is that this becomes a shared project. One source of truth. Everyone contributing improvements.

[pause]

The more campaigns we run through this, the more we learn. The more we standardize. The more the next campaign becomes easier.

[pause]

---

### The Integration Opportunities

There are handshakes we can build.

[pause]

With the Success Library - the definitions we create here can contribute content.

With the metadata catalog - the campaign configurations can inform the schema.

With automation - there's opportunity to schedule and streamline the refresh process. I heard Joseline has experience here.

With enterprise dashboards - aligning on format, cadence, ownership with the CIDM team.

[pause]

These aren't things I can decide or build alone. They're opportunities for the team.

[pause]

---

### What I Need From You

A few questions I'd welcome input on.

[pause]

How do we want to handle refresh cadence? How often do stakeholders need updated curves?

What secondary metrics are worth adding? What segment breakdowns would be valuable?

How should the two output tracks evolve? Does enterprise take over? Do we keep both?

[pause]

And - who wants to be involved? This is better as a team effort.

[pause]

---

### Close

**[Simple. Direct.]**

We deliver vintage curves. That's our service.

[pause]

This engine is one way to do it more consistently. More connected to the frameworks we're building. With more opportunity for the team to contribute and improve the process.

[pause]

That's what I wanted to share.

[pause]

Questions?

---
