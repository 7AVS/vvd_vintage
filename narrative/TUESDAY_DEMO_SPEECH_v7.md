# Vintage Curves Automation: Team Demo Speech (Version 7)

> **Duration:** ~15 minutes
> **Audience:** Team + directors
> **Generated:** 2026-01-26

## Full Spoken Delivery

---

**[Walk to position. Settle. Eye contact. Begin with intention.]**

Every campaign we run generates knowledge.

[pause]

Success definitions. Business rules. Filter logic. Measurement parameters.

And every time... that knowledge disappears into a notebook. Into someone's code. Into tribal memory.

[pause]

What I want to show you today is a different approach.

[pause]

---

### The Vintage Engine

This is a vintage curves engine. It calculates cumulative success rates over time. Lift. Confidence intervals. The measurement our team delivers.

[pause]

But here's what makes this different.

[pause]

The engine is built on a modular architecture - four layers, each independent. And it's designed to integrate with the Super Fact framework Roy introduced.

[pause]

**Layer one: Experiment Metadata.** Who is in the test. Treatment assignments. Cohort structure.

**Layer two: Campaign Metadata.** What we're measuring. Timing windows. Metric definitions.

**Layer three: Success Definitions.** How we calculate success. The business logic.

**Layer four: Client Journey.** What customers actually did. Email engagement. Channel response. Outcomes.

[pause]

The first and last layers pull from existing data sources. Stable. Consistent.

The middle layers - Campaign Metadata and Success Definitions - are where the logic lives. And right now, that logic is hardcoded.

[pause]

---

### The Design Decision

I want to be direct about where we are.

[pause]

Stage One. Current state. Hardcoded definitions. I configure each campaign manually.

This was intentional. A working system that produces output... is more valuable than a perfect architecture that doesn't run.

[pause]

But the architecture anticipates what comes next.

[pause]

Stage Two: the engine pulls definitions from the Success Library. From the metadata catalog. Dynamic configuration.

Stage Three: curated data sets. Fully automated pipeline.

[pause]

The engine remains stable across all three stages. What changes is where the definitions come from.

[pause]

---

### What's Running Today

Six campaigns are live. VCN, VDA, VDT, VUI, VUT, VAW.

Four success metrics are defined and calculating.

[pause]

Two delivery tracks.

Track A: enterprise integration with the dashboard team. In progress.

Track B: an in-house visualization. Deployed. Functional. Available now.

[pause]

---

### The Deeper Value

**[Slower pace. This is the insight.]**

Now. Here's what I find most interesting about this work.

[pause]

Every campaign that runs through this engine... captures something.

[pause]

When I define card acquisition for VCN - the filters, the tables, the business rules - that definition is documented. Structured. Reusable.

When I configure the measurement window for VDA - that parameter is recorded.

When I specify what wallet provisioning means for VUT - that logic is preserved.

[pause]

The curves are the output. The deliverable.

But underneath... we're building a knowledge base.

[pause]

Success definitions that can feed the Success Library. Campaign metadata that can populate the catalog. Business rules that become auditable, traceable, shareable.

[pause]

This isn't the primary purpose of the engine. The primary purpose is vintage curves.

But as a consequence of doing that work systematically... we generate assets.

[pause]

And those assets compound.

[pause]

---

### What Comes Next

There are decisions that shape where this goes. And I don't think they're mine to make alone.

[pause]

**Governance.** How do we formalize the handshake with the Success Library? What's the process for contributing definitions?

**Automation.** What's the refresh cadence? Who owns the trigger? Is there opportunity to schedule this?

**Prioritization.** Which secondary metrics matter? What segment breakdowns would be valuable?

**Track alignment.** How do the two delivery tracks evolve? Does one become primary?

[pause]

These are questions for the team.

[pause]

---

### Collaboration

Akash is building the GitHub repository for our team. This project will live there.

The intention is a centralized codebase. A single source of truth. Where contributions are shared and improvements benefit everyone.

[pause]

I'll document the architecture. The configuration patterns. The extension points.

And I'll make the onboarding accessible. Because not everyone has been working with these tools - and that's fine. We'll figure it out together.

[pause]

---

### Close

**[Return to the opening frame.]**

Every campaign we run generates knowledge.

[pause]

This engine makes sure that knowledge is captured. Structured. Ready to be reused.

[pause]

The vintage curves are the service we deliver.

The documented logic... is how we get better at delivering it.

[pause]

Thank you. I'd welcome your questions.

[pause]

---

## The One Thing

**If they remember nothing else:**

> "The vintage curves are the output. The documented logic - success definitions, business rules, campaign parameters - is how we get better at delivering them. Every campaign captures knowledge."

## Tone & Style Notes

| Aspect | v7 Approach |
|--------|-------------|
| **Opening** | Not "hi guys" - starts with the idea |
| **Pace** | Measured. Deliberate pauses. Lets ideas land. |
| **Structure** | Clear sections. Signposted transitions. |
| **Confidence** | States things directly. "I want to be direct." |
| **Humility** | "These are questions for the team." |
| **Close** | Returns to opening frame. Full circle. |
