## The Balance

| Aspect | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Vintage curves (the service) | **Primary** - this is what we do |
| Data assets (the hidden value) | **Secondary** - the quiet win, the hint |

**Why this balance:**
- We're a data analytics team, not data governance
- Curves are the deliverable everyone expects
- Data assets is YOUR insight as someone who understands governance value
- Don't oversell the secondary - let it be discovered
